# KL street reference photos

All photos come from Wikimedia Commons, scaled to 1280 px wide. Licences are as noted on each File: page.

- `jalan_tar_art_deco_shophouses_zebra_crossing.jpg` — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jalan_Tuanku_Abdul_Rahman_Art_Deco_Buildings.JPG — Bearsmalaysia / CC BY-SA 3.0 — teaches: Art Deco shophouse fronts in saturated candy colours (lemon yellow, bubblegum pink, mid grey) with stepped parapets and vertical fins; black/white striped kerb, white zebra bars on asphalt, steel bollards, traffic light on a short pole, tree planted in the pavement. Signboards are full-lot-width bands at first-floor level.
- `jalan_tar_row_of_art_deco_shophouses.jpg` — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Row_of_five_art-deco_shophouses,_Jalan_Tuanku_Abdul_Rahman_KL_21_March_2014.JPG — Derkommander0916 / CC BY-SA 3.0 — teaches: Five 3-storey art deco lots side by side, each ~5-6 m wide; faded cream/grey render vs one mint/orange repaint; signboards (green, red, yellow) span the lot width just above the five-foot way, plus a giant vinyl banner covering a whole facade. Glass curtain-wall tower right behind: old/new contrast is the KL look.
- `jalan_tar_monorail_yellow_box_junction.jpg` — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jalan_Tuanku_Abdul_Rahman,_Kuala_Lumpur_20230813_123218.jpg — Wiki Farazi / CC0 — teaches: Concrete monorail viaduct running down the street centre on T-piers; yellow box-junction hatch and yellow edge lines on grey asphalt; GrabFood/foodpanda riders with green/pink cube boxes; low 2-3 storey shophouses under 1980s concrete blocks.
